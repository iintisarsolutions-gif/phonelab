Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers Before You Arrange a Repair.

For the latest information on availability, repair assessment, turnaround and warranty coverage, please contact our team.

How long does a phone repair take?
Turnaround depends on the repair complexity, device condition, parts availability and service availability. Nationwide delivery may take approximately 2–3 days, while Klang Valley pickup and delivery may return repaired devices within approximately 1–3 hours where applicable.
Do you provide nationwide delivery service?
Yes. Customers across Malaysia can enjoy FREE delivery service. Please contact our team to confirm the current service process and availability for your location.
Do you offer Klang Valley pickup and delivery?
Yes. FREE pickup and delivery service is available for customers in the Klang Valley, subject to location and service availability. Turnaround may vary depending on the repair requirements.
Is there a warranty for repairs?
Warranty support of up to 6 months is available according to the applicable repair service and coverage terms. Please contact our team for the latest warranty information for your specific repair.
Will my personal data remain private?
Please discuss any data privacy concerns with our team before repair. We recommend backing up important data whenever possible and removing sensitive information where practical before handing over a device.
Can I walk in without a booking?
You may contact our team to check current availability before visiting. Booking in advance can help the team understand your device issue and service requirements.
How is the repair price determined?
Pricing varies depending on the device brand, model, condition and specific repair requirements. The listed starting prices are indicative; please contact our team for an accurate quotation.
What parts are used for repairs?
Repair solutions may use genuine or high-grade spare parts depending on the service and device requirement. Please contact our team for details about the available part options for your specific model.
How do I book a repair?
Click any “Book a Repair” button, complete the booking form and send the structured enquiry through WhatsApp. Our team can then confirm availability and estimated pricing.
How do I collect my repaired device?
You can collect your device in-store or use an applicable pickup and delivery option. Please confirm the preferred delivery method and current availability with our team.
